OSKILIFTS

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026 · Contact: ram_jhawar@berkeley.edu

This policy explains what OSKILIFTS collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account: your @berkeley.edu email address and a hashed password.
  • Profile: your username and display name.
  • Workouts: the workouts, exercises, sets, reps, notes, and dates you log, and each workout’s visibility setting.
  • Social activity: who you follow, who follows you, likes, comments you write, blocks, and reports you submit.
  • Account visibility: whether your account is Public or Private, and, if you tap “I’m heading to the RSF”, a temporary status that expires on its own (within 3 hours).
  • Gym usage data: the app also shows public RSF occupancy and class information; the basketball check-in feature uses an anonymous per-device identifier.

Who can see your information

Your username and display name are visible to other signed-in OSKILIFTS users. Accounts are Private by default: your workouts are visible only to you and to followers you have approved, unless you mark a workout Private, in which case only you can see it. If you switch your account to Public, your profile, your followers and following lists, and the workouts you share are visible to every signed-in OSKILIFTS user and can appear on the Explore feed. Your “heading to the RSF” status is visible only to friends (people you follow who also follow you). Your email address is never shown to other users.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database and authentication), Vercel (web hosting), and Render (server hosting) to run OSKILIFTS. They process data on our behalf.

We don’t sell your data

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can switch your account between Public and Private, mark workouts Private, remove followers, block users, and delete your account in the app. Deleting your account removes your profile, workouts, follows, likes, and comments. Reports you submitted about others, and a copy of content that was reported, may be kept to enforce our rules.

Contact

Questions or requests: ram_jhawar@berkeley.edu.
